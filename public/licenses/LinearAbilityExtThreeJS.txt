AbyssFieldMaterial.js concepts and the required simplex/fbm noise helpers are
copied or substantially adapted in src/abilities/kraken/.

The supplied older IceAbility.js, IceMaterial.js, settings.ice, createCrystalGeometry
and required noise helpers are also adapted in src/abilities/frostLance/.
Reference snapshots: frost-lance-donor-sources/. These user-supplied restored copies
are not a byte-for-byte verified Git commit. The donor MIT notice below covers this port.

in src/abilities/sandReaper/, inspired by procedural VFX engineering in
